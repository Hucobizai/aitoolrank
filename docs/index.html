How we evaluate

Listings describe products and link to official sources. They are not hands-on endorsements. We do not assign performance scores without repeatable tests and supporting evidence. Prices and features can change; verify details with the vendor.

Our future scoring rubric: task performance (35%), value (20%), ease of use (15%), reliability (15%), integrations (10%), and support (5%). Scores will be published only after testing.

Affiliate policy: If affiliate links are introduced, they will be clearly disclosed and will not determine editorial rankings.
